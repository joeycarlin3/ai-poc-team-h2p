# Quick Start Guide
### Welcome to the AI Interview Practice Tool!

This application is designed to help you prepare for interviews by providing instant, detailed, AI-powered feedback on your recorded answers. It evaluates your response for clarity, content relevance, tone, and confidence, and even suggests a better answer.

This guide will show you how to complete the core process:

1. Select a question and prepare.

2. Upload your recorded answer.

3. Review the Coach's Feedback.

---

### Task 1: Choose a Question and Upload
1. Choose a Question: At the top of the screen, click the dropdown menu next to "Choose an interview question." Select the question you practiced and recorded (e.g., "Tell me about yourself" or "What are your strengths?").
2. Record and upload your file : Record yourself answering the question and Locate the "Drag and drop file here" box under "1. Upload Your Recorded Answer."
    - Option A: Drag your recorded audio file from your computer directly into this box.
    - Option B: Click the "Browse files" button on the right to manually select the file.
3. Wait for Transcription: The application will show the file name and size (e.g., Tell Me About Yourself.wav). Wait a moment for the status message "Transcription Complete!" to appear.
4. Start Analysis: Click the large, red button labeled "Analyze Answer."

Note: You will see a message, "Step 2/2: Getting AI feedback..." Please wait—this step takes a moment to process the transcription and generate the detailed report.

---

### Task 2 Review the Coach's Feedback
This is where you get all the insights to improve your interviewing skills.
1. Once the analysis is complete, the results will appear below the "Analyze Answer" button, starting with a section titled "Coach's Feedback."
2. Read the Sections: Scroll down to review the structured feedback, which includes:
    - Executive Summary: A high-level overview of your performance.
    - Clarity & Structure (Focus): Evaluation of how well-organized your answer was.
    - Content & Relevance: Assessment of whether you included strong, relevant details.
    - Tone & Confidence: Analysis of the language and feel of your response.
    - Suggested Better Answer: A refined, high-scoring version of the response to help you understand how to phrase your next attempt.
    - Score out of 10: The final score given to your recorded answer.

Study the Suggested Answer: Review the section titled "Suggested Better Answer." This provides a refined, high-scoring version of the response to help you understand how to phrase your next attempt.

---

You can now use these detailed notes to re-record your answer and try the process again!
